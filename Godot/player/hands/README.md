# Optical hand meshes

These Godot humanoid hand meshes come from the official OpenXR hand tracking
demo at revision `3e08537616661a5883831628decab4c526260289`:
https://github.com/godotengine/godot-demo-projects/tree/3e08537616661a5883831628decab4c526260289/xr/openxr_hand_tracking_demo/assets/gltf

The demo assets are MIT licensed; see LICENSE.md. Their bone names and axes
match Godot XRHandModifier3D, including the hand root and finger tips. Keep the
original skin, inverse bind matrices, texture, and triangle winding together.
